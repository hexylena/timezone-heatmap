In my time

A global view of a given timepoint. Your browser provided timezone is highlighted as RED outlined ROW. The event timepoint is highlighted as a GREEN COLUMN.

Light grey cells indicate the 'usual' working hours (local / cultural traditions may deviate from assumed values. Consult your colleagues!)

Heavily inspired by WorldTimeBuddy.
